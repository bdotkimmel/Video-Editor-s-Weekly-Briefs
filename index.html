B129 · Targeted light for chronic congestion

Production direction · B129

Audience and hook: Cristian: this is a very TOF ad. The viewer knows the frustration of congestion but has no idea what light therapy is. The opening should make them care enough to keep watching: curiosity over clarity, tension over information, and emotion over lectures. Let the explanation unfold after the hook.

Format and mute test: Voiceover + B-roll. Cristian should be able to watch the full edit on mute and still understand the problem, what each light is being described as doing, and the intended breathing benefit. Use legible captions, short visual labels, and a clear sequence of problem → blue light → red light → easier breathing. Each shot should demonstrate the line being spoken.

Light-sequence visuals: Use a consistent, clearly labelled illustrative nasal cross-section. Establish the bacteria and swollen tissue first. For the blue-light passage, show blue light reaching the bacteria, with the bacteria visibly reducing. Then transition to red light for the tissue passage, show a simple labelled cellular-energy / ATP inset, and show the swollen tissue reducing with more space for airflow. Use distinct blue and red colours, readable labels, and airflow arrows so the two roles are immediately distinguishable. Keep these as illustrations of the script’s proposed explanation, rather than clinical footage or measured product results. Show the actual NovaFlow device accurately when it appears.

“Instead of constantly irritating them”: Show two split-screen B-roll clips: someone blowing their nose and someone picking their nose, to visually convey irritating or bothering the nose.

“Or relying on temporary fixes”: Show two split-screen B-roll clips: someone using nasal spray and someone taking allergy medication.

Core desire: Breathe more easily without constantly managing congestion.

Who: Top-of-funnel people who struggle with recurring congestion and have no idea what light therapy is. They recognise the breathing problem, but they don’t already understand this solution.

Angle: Create curiosity about a different approach to chronic congestion, then connect the light explanation to easier breathing and its everyday importance.

Journey: Chronic congestion → blue-light explanation → red-light explanation → impact on daily life → social proof → guarantee → purchase invitation.
